The studio's belief

Perfection makes imperfection work.

In our lounge wall there are sixty stones, and not one of them is straight, except one. A single clean triangle, set among the broken pieces. It holds the whole wall together. Every rough edge around it stops being an accident and becomes a choice. This is how we build: not hiding the hand that made it, but letting one true line give meaning to everything it touches.

The Studio / Udaipur

A workspace built of mud, stone and hands

Udaipur · Rajasthan

Our own studio is the argument made physical: walls finished in mud clay and cow dung, a lounge wall of raw stone carrying the brass अद्वैत, cane lamps woven by hand, and a coffee table carved from a single block of Indian marble. Wabi-sabi, folded into Rajasthan.

LocationUdaipur, Rajasthan
TypologyDesign Studio
ScopeArchitecture & Interiors
How the space was created

Designing our own workspace was the most personal brief we have taken. It had to be more than an office. It had to hold our philosophy in its walls: that design is an evolving narrative, not a static creation, and that a space should feel warm, earthy, and honest about the hands that made it.

The walls are finished in a blend of mud clay and cow dung, age-old techniques that regulate temperature, purify the air, and leave the faint scent of earth in the room. The lounge wall is raw stone, dozens of broken pieces holding one clean triangle, with the ADVAIT name in brush gold at its heart. It is wabi-sabi folded into Rajasthan's own heritage: imperfection, made to work.

Every detail honours the artisan. The coffee table is hand-carved from a single block of Indian-mined marble, its chiselled grooves left visible. The cane lamps above are woven by hand, throwing warm, patterned shadows across the lounge. Even the plaster carries a carved jharokha: Udaipur, pressed into the wall itself.

The corridor holds our story in frames, features from Elle Decor India and Architect and Interiors India, leading to the conference room, where a globe pendant lights the table our projects are shaped on. The studio is not a finished project; it grows with us, and it reminds us daily that architecture is not about structures, but about the experience of standing inside one.

Girikunj

Private Residence, Udaipur

Girikunj is a home whose façade folds like origami, opening the interiors to light and movement. The journey begins at a cascading water wall by the porch and carries through spaces shaped by natural materials and careful craft. Playful, personal touches, like a hand-painted panel above a child's bed, give each room its own character. The result is a warm, contemporary home that feels grounded and alive.

Shivarti House of Mewar

Private Residence, Udaipur

Set on a sprawling 7,000 sq. ft. plot, Shivarti is a neo-classical bungalow that celebrates the lineage of a royal Rajasthani family. An elegant palette of beige and white, fluted columns, and refined craftsmanship give it a sense of timeless grandeur. It gently bridges generations, letting age-old customs and contemporary aspirations share one roof.

Aikyam

Private Residence, Udaipur

Aikyam is a 3,700 sq. ft. home designed for a multigenerational family who wanted warm, nature-inspired contemporary spaces. Its architecture plays with masses and voids, while a recurring language of lines runs through the staircase, flooring, grills, and walls. The result is a home that feels both visually stimulating and quietly welcoming.

Mehta Residency

Residence

Mehta Residency is a contemporary home shaped by warmth and quiet continuity. A refined palette of soft beige, natural wood, muted blue, and charcoal feels sophisticated yet lived-in. Continuous light-toned stone flooring, threaded with subtle black inlays, flows through the spaces so the home feels open and expansive. Fluted details and gentle curves add character while keeping everything calm.

Earthen Nest

Residence

Earthen Nest is a contemporary sanctuary rooted in natural, vernacular living. Warm timber, earthy beige, natural stone, and terracotta set a calm, grounded mood. Lime-plastered walls, cane, carved wood, and gentle arches celebrate the hand of the artisan. Light, texture, and the landscape flow through every room, making a home built for slow, meaningful days.

Modern Casa

Residence

Modern Casa is a contemporary home designed to feel warm and effortlessly livable. Natural wood, fluted panels, and stone-inspired surfaces meet matte black and rich teal for a balanced, sophisticated palette. Curved forms and bespoke furniture add depth while keeping the look clean and uncluttered. The result is a timeless, layered home full of quiet character.

Samgrathanam

Residence, Udaipur

Samgrathanam is a bungalow where tradition, modernity, and craft come together around family life. Its facade, inspired by woven sweater patterns carved into warm mocha-cream stone, gives the home its identity. Inside, the spaces move between playful and opulent, from carved marble backdrops to a parametric family lounge. A minimal yet warm palette and precise detailing make it deeply personal and quietly elegant.

The Luxury Tiger Hotel

Hotel

The Luxury Tiger is a contemporary space warmed by subtle traditional Indian influences. Rich woods, brass accents, natural stone, and handcrafted detail build a refined, tactile palette. Soft neutral tones, custom furniture, and layered lighting keep it calm and elegant. The result balances modern comfort with timeless craftsmanship.

Navlakha Resort

Resort

Navlakha Resort is designed as a place to slow down and feel at ease from the moment you arrive. Natural materials and a palette of soft sage, ivory, and walnut create a refined yet relaxed retreat. Calm, curved bedrooms give way to more expressive living spaces with statement furniture and sculptural pieces. Brass accents and layered textures bring an understated sense of luxury.

Jito Udaipur Office

Office, Udaipur

Designed for the Jain International Trade Organisation, this workspace pairs precision with understated warmth. A calm palette of soft whites, warm greys, oak, and charcoal is lifted by vibrant brand colour in art and signage. Fluted surfaces, glass partitions, polished stone, and timber give it depth and character. The result is a professional space that still feels distinctly human.

Office for Dazzle Marbles

Office, Udaipur

The Office for Dazzle Marble blends modern design with traditional, sustainable materials. Fluidic accent walls shift from sharp marble flutes to the organic curves of lime plaster, echoing the owner's trade in stone. Teak and cane furniture with brass inlay meet an earthy palette that complements their Dazzle marble. Natural light and eco-conscious materials make it a workspace with warmth and integrity.

Display Centre for Imperial Impex

Display Centre, Urban Square Mall, Udaipur

This display centre pairs modern sophistication with a raw, minimalist mood. A neutral palette balances the owner's trade in quartz and exotic stone, letting the materials take centre stage. Metal-frame glass partitions bring openness while holding acoustic and visual privacy, and considered vignettes display the products. Warm wooden tones ground the metal-and-stone dynamic throughout.

Dona Kitchen Store

Kitchen Showroom

The DONA luxury kitchen showroom is a study in refined craftsmanship and contemporary luxury. It sets two kitchens in dialogue: one bold in dark stone, wood, and brass, the other light in marble, fluted detail, and soft neutrals. Statement lighting, custom cabinetry, and sculptural elements give the space warmth and presence. It is a showroom where premium materials and fine detail meet.

Crystal Couture

Boutique, Panchwati, Udaipur

Crystal Couture is a boutique conceived around timeless elegance. Classic elements meet modern touches to create a sophisticated, inviting space for its curated fashion collection. A neutral palette of creamy whites, warm beige, and soft grey sets a calm, refined backdrop. Considered lighting lets the colours and details of the merchandise take centre stage.
